01 / Profile & Philosophy

Driven by Curiosity.
Inspired by Innovation.

As a Computer Engineering student, I enjoy exploring how technology, logical thinking, and creativity can work together to solve real-world problems. Through academic projects and hands-on learning, I continue to develop my technical abilities, strengthen my problem-solving skills, and discover new possibilities in technology.

My academic journey at ICCT Colleges has instilled in me a deep appreciation for both sides of modern computing: the concrete physical world of electronic circuits and digital logic, alongside the structured realm of software architecture and human-centered design.

Whether modeling relational databases for clinic workflows, prototyping intuitive interfaces in Figma, or analyzing logic circuits on a breadboard, my goal is to design solutions that are practical, ethical, and built with genuine care for the people who rely on them.
